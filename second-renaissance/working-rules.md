# Second Renaissance Book

The book on the Second Renaissance and Second Enlightenment, and the mission underneath it: helping heal the great split, between the two hemispheres (the McGilchrist wound) and between religion and science. This is mission work, served by the agentic research engine (see [[agentic-org-is-mission-research-engine]]), not a product.

> New session: read `00-the-book.md` first (the living spine, v0.1). It holds the thesis-as-currently-held (for Michael to correct, not defend), the provisional arc, the knowledge model, the source spine, and the interview agenda.

## What this is
A serious book built on primary sources, written in Michael's voice, that names the great split as the root of the civilisation crisis and the re-integration as the way through. Companion, not duplicate, to `Building the OS Book/` (that one is the how of agentic-first organisations; this one is the why beneath the whole mission).

## How we work on it
- **Interview-first.** Michael thinks out loud; I let him wander, then extract and structure. The arc in `00-the-book.md` is provisional scaffolding to react against, never a fixed outline. He updates positions, doesn't defend them.
- **Mirror truth, tag every claim.** No claim goes in unsourced. Each carries its provenance (tier + source) per [[provenance-and-traceability]]. Architecture is not proof; a beautiful structure is not a finished argument.
- **Voice.** Warm, heartfelt, fellow traveller, not guru. No AI-speak, no listicles, no em dashes, no absolute-truth claims. The reader should finish in *wonder*, not just informed. Full voice: `memory/context/voice-profile.txt`.
- **One thread at a time.** Scope discipline. We go deep on one chapter / one question per session.
- **Growth-edge partner.** Push Michael to stretch; note open questions and tensions; suggest unwalked paths and thinkers he hasn't reached for (hold the full influence web, never default to 3-4 names).

## The knowledge model (provenance × maturity)
Two axes, both required before anything is "canon":
- **Source provenance (standard vault tiers):** T1 primary (Source Library original scans + its cited AI translations; the curated 228+) · T2 institutional · T3 secondary analysis (books, essays) · T4 journalistic · T5 own synthesis (our drafts) · T6 conversation (Plaud lectures, spoken).
- **Claim maturity (the book's own assertions):** draft → confirmed (sourced + verified) → **canon-locked** (T0, changed only via an explicit Amendment note, mirroring the OS book's Canon discipline).
