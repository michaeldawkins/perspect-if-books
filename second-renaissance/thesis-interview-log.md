# The Second Renaissance: thesis interview log

Running record of the thesis interview. Building from Ex Fontes (the 13-03-2026 source document plus the 30-06-2025 three-act version). Maturity: draft, confirmed (Michael said it), or settled (Michael locked it).

## Q1: the one-sentence thesis (14-11-2026)

**Drafted by Claude:** "Our crises are one crisis, a split between mind and nature, reason and soul. The wisdom from before that split was set aside, not disproven. And the tools we are building now will either deepen the split or help heal it, depending on the consciousness of the people who build them."

**Claude pushback:** Act II of Ex Fontes leans on psi, morphic fields and precognition as the cracks in the old worldview. That is the weakest ground for a skeptical reader.

**Michael (confirmed):** the strongest foundation is Bernardo Kastrup's idealism. It gives scientists a strong, rational basis to take the position that consciousness is primary.

**Claude notes:** Kastrup's name for it is *analytic idealism*. It is a minority position in academic philosophy, so if it is the foundation, the book must argue for it, not assume it. Open: is consciousness-first the thesis itself, or the floor beneath it?

**Status:** foundation confirmed (consciousness is primary, via Kastrup). Thesis sentence still draft v2.

**Draft v2:** "Consciousness is not a by-product of matter but the ground of reality. We built a civilisation on the opposite assumption, and our crises are what that mistake looks like at scale. The wisdom from before the split knew this, and the tools we build now will carry whichever assumption we build them from."

## Q2: idealism as thesis or as floor, and who is the reader (14-11-2026)

**Michael (confirmed):** option B. The thesis is that the split is the root of our crises; idealism (Kastrup) is the deepest floor that explains why the split is false. The audience is Gen Z.

**Consequence:** the GenZ outline (Dropbox, 20-05-2025) becomes the structural backbone, with Ex Fontes as the strongest prose and the third way manifesto as the frame (three paths).

**Claude pushback:** the existing drafts are written for a different reader. Ex Fontes speaks to the Embassy of the Free Mind crowd; the thinker lists (Kastrup, McGilchrist, Schmachtenberger) are dense for a 20-year-old. Gen Z will smell a lecture instantly. Open: who is Michael to this reader?

**Draft v3 thesis (tuned for Gen Z):** "The anxiety you feel is not a personal malfunction. It is the honest response to a world built on a mistake: that we are separate from each other, from nature, and that consciousness is a by-product of dead matter. That mistake can be unlearned, and your generation, holding the most powerful tools ever made, gets to decide which story they serve."

**Status:** B confirmed, audience confirmed. Thesis sentence still draft v3.
