Personal Manifesto Michael J. Dawkins



I believe that we are on the verge of a new era. An era that will be a testimony to humanities true essence. I believe now is the time for transformation to a world, universe even, being in harmony.

I believe that we only get those challenges in life that we need to learn, grow and evolve. In order to learn, grow and evolve we should not shy away from these challenges. They might be daunting or outright frightening at times. If so, know you/we are on the right path. Who said change has to be easy?

“Do Not Go Where The Path May Lead; Go Instead Where There Is No Path And Leave A Trail.”

Embrace your fear, embrace the challenge. Be courageous, be bold, be the change and above all else be loving and kind. To yourself and the rest of the world.

Nearly all the problems, hurt, pain and suffering we see in our society and world today, are human problems. We created them. If this is true, wow, what have we done! Perhaps it’s painful to recognize and bear. But bear with me, if this is true, it also means we are the solution. That we have what it takes to heal & transform our society and planet.

What you see in the world today is a reflection of our inner reality. So if we want to change the world we need to first change our inner-world. Change does indeed start within. What is the story you are telling yourself? What is it that you believe to be true? What is your narrative? Our stories and beliefs our own reality. The stories we share and believe in together create our society.

So let’s change the story

Let’s change the story and take responsibility of our story for the new world into our own hands. Utilizing all the means that the universe provides. As we have always done. But now from a higher state of consciousness. The state of consciousness where we experience a deep sense of knowing that we are all one. One with everything, and no-thing, that ever was and will be. Let us as human beings take up arms of love and kindness in the face of all adversity. In name of every living being and realize our true human potential and collective purpose of being.

“We cannot solve our problems with the same thinking we used when we created them” – Albert Einstein
