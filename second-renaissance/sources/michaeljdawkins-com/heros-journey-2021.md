**Michael J. Dawkins’s Hero’s Journey**

 

My own path of awakening to fulfill my life’s purpose started around 2006\. With one of my biggest gifts, a burn-out. And I was only 26\! Back then, a burn-out wants as prevalent as it is now. I felt like a failure. As a misfit. But in in hindsight, it was one of the biggest gifts that I was bestowed upon.

“it’s no measure of good health to be well adjusted to a profoundly sick society.”

It set me on a path of self-discovery and actualization. I was able to break free of the image of who I thought I should be. Living a life out of alignment with my true essence. And start to wonder and wander. Not knowing and being curious to what may emerge.

One thing that was glaringly obvious, is that the pursuit of happiness, of becoming happy, started with being happy. My old way, of doing things that I didn’t want to do, but I thought would make me happy in the future, became obsolete. I was living in the future, doing what I thought I should be doing to become successful. And thus happy. Or I was living in the past, looking to all my mistakes. I was trying to be the perfect son which my parents could be proud of.

This fixed image in my mind was all in my own imagination. It drove me to only do those things that I knew I would be successful in. I started my studies well and got through the first year with ease. In that year I had realized that I did not want to become a physical therapist, but I was now 18 months into the degree and thought, just as well finish it and then do something else. Only 2,5 years to go…Because stopping now would mean giving up. And giving up would mean failure. And being a failure was the last thing I wanted to be. So, I went on, but without passion. And pushed on, but struggled. After six years of struggling. I chose to drop out and start my career. I was already 24 and not yet successful.

How could I be successful and not feel as if these 6 years had gone to waste? Become a pharmaceutical representative. I could earn a good income and climb the career ladder. With my first wage I was able to get a mortgage and buy a house and in so doing climb the ladder of success. However, life had something else planned for me. After just two years and two different pharmaceutical companies later, I was burn-out at just 26 years of age. It wasn’t just the highly competitive environment that led to the burn-out. It was the 7 years of struggling, not doing what I was passionate about or made me happy, that led to it.

I was “successful” by societies standards, even though I was a school dropout. I had a good job, I had a great salary, bonus structure, company car, spending account and a house. I should be happy right? But I wasn’t.

I was working hard and playing hard. I was fit but not healthy to say the least. I was drinking, smoking and enjoying other state altering substances. All to help cope with not living my true essence. And from the outside at least, it was all working. Until it all came crashing down and I couldn’t hold up the façade of being successful anymore.

I went through a dark period. My partying hard shifted to full on substance abuse to numb reality. I can only imagine what this did to my loved ones around me. To see me self-descruct in this way. But in hindsight, It was all needed, to hit rock bottom. To deconstruct my “self” in order to rebuild my true self. Deconstructing my self-image, my ego, my beliefs. Creating space for my true self to start emerging. Starting my hero’s journey.

And now fast forward 14 years. What Have I learned? What is the “boon” I want to share with the world? And how can I bring this to life in order to carry on with my hero’s journey and realize my true potential? This is two-fold. First I want to create an environment where game changers, changemakers and edge walkers can come together. Come together and (re)energize. To find a place in the world, a community or tribe that feels like home. To rest our perhaps weary bones and replenish our vigor. To share our adventures and discoveries. To feel comradery in our endeavors to change the world. Knowing that this is arduous, painstaking work. One person at a time. Always starting with me (and you).

To learn how to optimize our energy. How to cultivate hope. Heal our past to realize a future that is in harmony with itself and its environment. To create a world teaming with life. Realized through love and compassion.

“if it takes a village to raise a child, it takes a global ecosystem to change the world”

And it’s my beliefe we all have a part to play. And that there is no thing like a small part or role. Think of the butterfly effect. No one is exonerated, ever. Everyone can do something, together we can do anything. Let that anything be realizing a world that….

When? Is now perhaps a good moment to start? To start with energizing? We all know of the metaphor that we should first helop ourselves in an airplane crash putting on the oxygen mask. But do we in real life? And if we do, do we then tend to others? Help them put on their proverbial oxygen mask? What if we would all do this? What kind of world could we then realize, together? Can you see it? Can you feel it? Can you imagine it?

And in second place I want to realize a project that helps heal not just us as human beings but our planetary ecosystem. We have lived and are living a life of separation. However, everything is interconnected. As within, as without. As above as below. If this principle is true, what we do to our planet, we do onto ourselves. We have been depleting the planet of her finite resources and life for that matter. We are on the verge of the 6th mass extitinction. The loss of biodiversity just over the last 50 years is shocking to say the least. Not the least in our soil and all its microbes.

Over the last 50 years we have also seen a epidemic of lifestyle diseases. And a correlating decline in our microbiome diversity. We are just uncovering the important role of these micro biota in regards to our overall health.

We are what we eat is a know adagio. However it goes a bit deeper then that. If we are what we eat, we are also what our food eats. And that’s where we start to unpack what really lies at the heart of our ecosystem and health crisis. Through focusing on feeding the planet and optimizing profitability. We have created industrial agriculture. That has indeed realized an abundance of macronutrients. Unfortunately, this has led to mall nutrition on a very large scale. In some countries people still don’t have enough food, in other countries, there is an abundance of food. Well perhaps we should call this food like edible substances. Because these lack the micronutrients. So you could say. The world is in a micronutrient deficiency crisis.

We have lost the sight of the importance role of micronutrients and microbes in regards to our health and the planets health. We are depleting our topsoil faster then it can replenish itself. We are pumping in the compounds and minerals to let our food grow rapidly. Our soil health is depleting, as is our own health. What if we could create a new food system that nourishes our bodies, soil and soul? That’s my second focus.

So what is this “boon” I want to bring back in servitude to the world? What have I learned and discovered for myself over the past 14 years? In 2020 hindsight, yes, I realized this last year 😉 It’s easy to see how everything has been converging to this point in time for me to put all my learnings into concrete action. To put all that I have experienced to the test. To show up fully and express my true self, my true colors. To have the courage to expose myself for who I truly am. With no holding back. Daring greatly, with the risk of being chastised. But I feel that now is the time. The world has had a rude awakening. And people are gasping for a new normal. Let this new normal be that of more love and compassion for life. Life to flourish. Life to expand. Love of life leads to a life of love.

**My experiences**

I have been trained to become a physical therapist. I specialized in stress related disorders. Early on I realized that there is more to a human then just their so to say “nuts and bolts”. If I wanted to help mobilize someone’s shoulder, I also needed to “treat” the whole person. Their, mind, their beingness even. Afterall we are called human beings, not human do-ers.

At that time I was also working part time as a fitness instructor and had specialized as a medical training therapist. I loved that work, really connecting with people and guiding and helping them realize their fitness/health goals. Especially because they were intrinsically motivated.

And I was able to help them with my extensive knowledge of the human body and passion for fitness. I had this job from 1999 till 2005\. In those days I was extremely fit. Working out twice a day and lifting ridiculous amount of weights. I was fit, but I wouldn’t say I was healthy. Working hard, put also playing hard. I was so fanatical with fitness because I wasn’t pleased with who I was. And I tried to control life and sculpt my image to an unrealistic picture in my mind of who I thought I should be. Over time I developed a body dysmorphic syndrome called “Adonis complex”. This is the male version of anorexia. But then instead of wanting to be skinnier ever more, to muscle packed ever more. Me being in the surroundings of the fitness center for all those hours of the day, combined with identifying myself with my role as fitness instructor, led me to become ever more extreme. Not wanting to go on holiday because this would disrupt my training schedule and even resulting in the use of anabolic steroids. Luckily, I only did one course of them, as my inner voice was strong enough to pull myself away from that destructive path. This inner voice would serve me again and again in future endeavors. Pulling me back from certain endeavors, that were not serving me.

In 2003 I started a physical therapy business, called Physiocare. I loved setting up the business, but as mentioned, didn’t like the work as a therapist itself. It felt constricting and monotonous to me. Stuck in a room of only a few square meters and getting a lot of the same issues to work with. Back, knee or shoulder problems. Also I experienced that I was working harder then my patients. They didn’t (want to) take responsibility for their own health. They just wanted to get “fixed”. And it was “free”. After all, they had already paid into their insurance. By 2005….

 

Question 1

I had hit rock bottom. I was in a complete apathetic state. I had retracted into myself. Shutting everyone out, zoning out as much as I could. I laid on my couch for days and nights. Just watching whatever, it didn’t matter. As long as I could hide from reality. I didn’t get up for anything except for the toilet. I didn’t have any food and couldn’t muster the energy of going to the super market just down the road. I started to dream of eating. Finally after two nights and three days I mustered up all of my energy and courage I had left and did some grocery shopping with the last of my money.

A day later friends showed up at my doorstep. They hadn’t been able to reach me by telephone and had started to worry. They decided to travel 60 km just to check on me. And there they were. Knocking on my door. I was shocked. I didn’t want them here. I didn’t want them to see me like this. But I couldn’t not let them in. So I opened the door.

The action of opening the door, I now realize was a physical action, connected to a metaphysical level. Letting people in, not being able to hold up any appearance. Being completely vulnerable. I answered the call of the universe to start climbing up from the deep well of unhappiness, loneliness and victimhood I had been keeping myself in. I don’t know what would have happened to me if they hadn’t of shown up. To just be there for me in my biggest time of need. Without judgement. Only love and encouragement. They each gave me 50 euro’s so I could get more food. This act of loving kindness was the spark I needed to dust myself of and start getting back on track. I had a long way to go though…

It had become clear to me that money and status wasn’t important to me as I had thought. That it didn’t make me happy. I was at that time just midway my Master NLP training course at New Life University. One of the most impactful trainings I have undergone. It pulled on the loose hanging thread that my education had realized. A thread of a strong and thickly woven tapestry that was my belief structure, and completely unraveled it. I don’t know what was up or down, left or right anymore. I was in a state of utter confusion of what to believe was true. During this training I had also started reading. Or at least listening to audio books. I had an aversion to reading since high school and had told myself, I didn’t like to read (hahaha).

Due to all my learnings at that time it became clear to me that in order to become happy, I first had to be happy. What a complete mind-fuck and paradox. I had been living in unhappiness, thinking this was the price I had to pay in order to become happy. When I would have this or that…Reaching a certain end state.

I had come to realize that I was living in my head all of the time. Either in the past or in the future, mostly the future. But never in the present moment. The now as Eckhart Tolle puts it. I became really interested (hahaha) in the now. And I wanted to know what it was. I wanted to understand the now. But how hard I tried to understand it, the ever elusive it was. I asked my trainer at that time if he could help me understand. He answered by telling me top sit on a chair and feel the chair. What a ridiculous answer to my question I thought. How would this help me understand the now? I tried it but to no avail. My quest for the now took about six months and then it just happened. I was reading the power of now. I remember it was an arduous read for me until…it just clicked. It was as if a light switch had been turned on. I can remember it like it was yesterday. I had the flu quite severely at the time. I had even gone back to my parents. I was in bed and reading the book when the sentences all of a sudden actually made sense. It was as if they had been there waiting for me to shift in my awareness. And it had taken my illness to do so. There is no suffering in the now, I jumped out of bed and ran down the stairs into the garden. I raised my arm as if I had just won a race and shouted, I am in the now\!\!\! My mum looked at me a bit funny. She must have thought I was delirious because of my fever, but I was more clear than ever. It was my first conscious experience of the now.

This made it possible to practice being in the now, in the present moment more. Now the sitting and feeling the chair, that my trainer had mentioned, made sense 😉

I started walking in nature more. It was very therapeutic for my recovery from my burn-out. I was listening to Philosophers Notes from Brian Johnson. And also practicing noticing nature all around me. Wow, it was as if I was seeing it for the first time in my life. And it was, consciously that is. Wonder, amazement and appreciation filled my being.

A new book of Eckhart Tolle had come out the year before. The follow up of the Power of Now. I bought a copy as soon as I could and delved into it as soon as I got my hands on it. Yes, I had moved on to real books in stead of just audiobooks, wohoo 😉 Wow, I was sucked in and couldn’t put it down. It just resonated with me. It filled me with excitement and profound insights. It was as if it had been written just for me. It also gave me hope, hope for a better world. It also helped me realize that I perhaps wasn’t a failure, having a burn-out. I could start looking at it as one of the biggest gifts that I was ever given. The universe helping me to awaken to my lifes purpose. This is also the subtitle of the book 😉

My recovery took a lot longer than I had imagined. A total of 1,5 years before I was able to get back to a job. (now looking back, I actually never really recovered 😉 I became an account manager for a head hunting company. They had led me to believe they were there to help people realize their full potential and ultimate job. It became apparent very quickly that this wasn’t the case. I had shifted from a drug seller for the pharmaceutical industry, to a people trafficker. This was not what I had signed up for nor wanted\! Around this time I was just finishing my master NLP course and received a warm lead for a coaching client from a course buddy of mine. I remember the call I made to the company that this person worked for, like yesterday (story to come)

 

I had come to realize that I wanted, no I needed to make a meaningful contribution to the world. And now my contract wasn’t continued, I decided to start my own business again. I had come to the conclusion that I couldn’t function within the hierarchical structure of a corporation. That I just couldn’t be a cog in the machine. Especially if that machine was just out on making money, at any cost. Not really adding any value.

It wasn’t so much that I wanted to be an entrepreneur, it was the fact, that I didn’t see any other option to be able to be true to myself, and to have a meaningful contribution to the world.

It was made easier that I got my first coaching client. (story to come)
