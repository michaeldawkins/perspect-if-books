## The arc in one paragraph
A teenager who found in festivals and substances a love and connection he couldn't find in the "real world" ("I've been to the land of milk and honey"). A physio student who never finished, then a pharma sales manager who called himself a "drug seller". NLP (2004 to 2005) and Eckhart Tolle crack the door open. Then the burnout at 26: one room with no heating, a boss arriving with the police, three days on the couch, a year and a half to recover, and the question "Is this really it?" Out of that came Vipassana, energy work, and then twenty years of building outward (OpenCoffee, Duurzaam Brabant, SingularityU) and turning inward (healing, fatherhood, 18 kg lost at 40 for his daughter Ila). On 26-06-2023 he searched "bernardo kastrup idealism" and, by his own timeline, everything after that changes. From there: Essentia Foundation, the Faggin book launch, the Embassy of the Free Mind, Perspect-IF.

## Moments that could carry chapters
1. **The land of milk and honey** (around 15 to 20): seeking connection through substances. This is the doorway many Gen Z readers know.
2. **The burnout at 26** (2006 to 2008): "the death of the old operating system". Interview A calls it "the hinge of the entire biography".
3. **Vipassana at 27**: the inner rebel ("I should be out enjoying life") who stayed anyway.
4. **Mirror at 40**: 106 kg, a newborn daughter, and the decision to change.
5. **Peppa Pig and the double slit** (11-06-2023): watching cartoons with Ila, then searching for the experiments that broke materialism.
6. **Kastrup day** (26-06-2023): the foundation the book now stands on.
7. **The bridge was always there**: ancient wisdom and modern science carry equal weight across twenty years of the archive.
