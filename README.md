## What is settled and what is not

Each claim has a maturity:

- **Draft:** written by Claude or found in an old source. Something to push against, not to trust.
- **Confirmed:** Michael said it in an interview.
- **Settled:** Michael locked it. Changed only on purpose, with a dated note in the log.

The interview log is the record of what moved from draft to confirmed.

### The Second Renaissance, as of 14-11-2026
- **Confirmed:** the thesis is that the split (mind from nature, reason from soul) is the root of our crises. Consciousness being primary, with Kastrup's analytic idealism, is the deepest floor beneath it, not the opening claim.
- **Confirmed:** the reader is Gen Z.
- **Confirmed:** Michael writes as a fellow traveller a few steps ahead, on his own healing path since 2006. His backstory is a core part of the book.
- **Draft:** the one-sentence thesis (v3), the structure, the title.

## House rules
Plain language. No em dashes in new writing. Dates as DD-MM-YYYY. Lowercase-hyphen filenames. Outward text speaks of an organisation, not an organism. Files under `sources/` are kept as found, so they may break these rules.
